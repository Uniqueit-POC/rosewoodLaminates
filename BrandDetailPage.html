Built to Last

Long-Lasting Surface Protection

Scratch & Impact Protection

  • Don't place lighted cigarettes directly on laminate surface.

  • Knives or other sharp utensils may slice or scratch the surface.

  • Avoid ironing or placing a hot iron on laminate surface.

  • Heavy blows of hammer or metal may crack the laminate surface.

Ordinary Cleaning

  • Use a clean, damp, non-abrasive cotton cloth and a mild liquid detergent or household cleaner.

  • Rinse with clean water. Don't flood the laminate since water can be absorbed and results substrate to swell.

  • Dry the surface with a soft, clean and non-abrasive cotton cloth.

Chemical Damage

  • Never use cleaners containing acid, alkali or sodium hypochlorite.

  • These cleaners will mar, etch, corrode and permanently discolour the laminate surface.

  • Make sure bottles, rags and other materials contaminated with these cleaners never come in contact with the laminate surface.

Installation Guide

Direction of Use

Follow these steps for a perfect, long-lasting laminate installation every time.

1

Before using laminate please ensure that the surface of plywood and laminate are clean, dry and free of other contaminations.

2

Please well stir adhesive before use and don't add water in it. Also ensure that the applied adhesive is sufficient.

3

Once laminate is pasted on plywood surface, apply adequate pressure for perfect bonding. Use a roller press from centre of assembly towards the edge to remove air bubbles.

4

A gap of minimum 2 mm should be maintained between two laminates while pasting side by side.
